03 / About

Engineer, researcher, founder.

I'm Onyeka, a software engineer and researcher in Enugu, Nigeria. I build cross-platform apps for Android, Windows and Linux, plus developer tools and AI systems, mostly in Python and Flet.

I founded Kiri Research Labs, which builds lightweight software for edge intelligence: small tools that run on phones, low-memory servers and constrained networks.

I studied Agricultural and Bioresources Engineering at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka. That background still shapes how I think about systems and constraints.

Now
  • Shipping FFmpeg AV, Proot Distro X11 and Asase.
  • Building autonomous ingestion agents for Igbo Archives.
  • Open to engineering and research roles.
